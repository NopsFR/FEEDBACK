# FEEDBACK — Brand Guide

## The idea
Feedback is what happens when a signal goes round the loop and comes back louder. The brand is built from that one
moment: a flat carrier line that suddenly spikes. The line runs through the wordmark and replaces the A; on its own it
is the symbol.

FEEDBACK is a player for music you own. It lives next to band merch, gig flyers, scratched jewel cases and a cable coiled
at the end of the night — not next to dashboards.

**Values:** yours (local, no account) · loud (confident, physical) · worn-in (imperfect, used, kept) · honest (no fake
numbers, no hype).

## Name
Always `FEEDBACK`, all caps, one word. Never `Feed Back`, `FEED//BACK`, `F33DBACK`, or with the spike typed as a
character. In running text write FEEDBACK; the spike only appears in the drawn wordmark.
Do not add ® or ™ — the name is not registered. Run a trademark check before any public release.

## Logo system
Sources: `brand/logo/*.svg`, generated by `design/generators/brand_v2.py` (edit the generator, re-run, never hand-edit
exports).

| Asset | File | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Wordmark | `feedback-wordmark.svg` | Default everywhere there's room |
| Wordmark, worn | `feedback-wordmark-worn.svg` | Posters, merch, splash, marketing. Not below 240 px wide |
| Symbol | `feedback-symbol.svg` | Avatars, stamps, loading, favicon ≥ 32 px |
| Symbol, small | `feedback-symbol-small.svg` | 16–31 px |
| Emblem | `feedback-emblem.svg` | Disc stamps, stickers, CD centres |
| Icon glyph | `feedback-icon-glyph.svg` | App icons (fewer, fatter spikes) |
| Favicon glyph | `feedback-favicon-glyph.svg` | 16–32 px favicons, tray |
| Lockups | `brand/logo/lockups/*.svg` | Horizontal, stacked, tagline |

**Construction.** Letterforms start from Archivo at weight 900 / width 125 (SIL OFL), tracked −7 units, then modified:
the A is removed and replaced by an asymmetric 13-spike waveform whose tallest spike rises above the cap height; a
tapering hairline cut runs through D–B and the carrier line exits right of K and fades.

**Clear space.** The height of the E's middle bar on all sides (≈ 18% of cap height). Nothing enters that area,
including the spike's overshoot.

**Minimum sizes.** Wordmark 72 px / 18 mm wide. Worn wordmark 240 px / 50 mm. Symbol 32 px (use small symbol below
that). Favicon glyph 16 px.

**Colour.** Paper (#E6E1D6) on black, black (#0B0B0B) on paper, or accent red (#FF2A3A) on black for one-off moments.
Monochrome only — no gradients inside the mark. Chrome/metal treatments are for renders and photography, not for the
flat logo.

**Don't:** outline it, add glows, recolour individual letters, put it in a circle (except the emblem), stretch it,
typeset a new wordmark, replace the spike with a play button or a heart-rate line, animate spikes independently of
sound in marketing.

## App icon
Masters: `brand/icons/master/icon-{dark,red}-{rounded,square}.svg` → 1024 px PNGs via `design/generators/icon_render.py`.
- Primary: worn black tile, paper spike glyph, faint scratches, red light leak top-right, bevelled edge.
- Alternate: same tile with red glyph and glow (store/promo alternates only).
- `square` = full-bleed (iOS, Android adaptive background, PWA maskable). `rounded` = transparent margin (Windows, macOS, PWA any).
- Below 32 px the favicon glyph (5 spikes) is used — the 9-spike glyph turns to mush at 16 px.

text contrast never changes.

## Typography
| Role | Face | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Display | Archivo, width 125, weight 800–900, uppercase | Titles, album names, big numbers. Tight leading 0.86–0.95 |
| Labels | Archivo, width 112, weight 560–650, uppercase, tracking 0.16–0.18em, 9.5–11 px | Section heads, buttons |
| UI / body | Inter (variable) 400–560 | Everything readable |
| Detail | IBM Plex Mono 400/500 | Durations, counts, codec specs, section numbers |
| Handwritten | Covered By Your Grace | One short note per screen at most. Never for information |

All fonts are SIL Open Font License, bundled locally (no network).
Why not the "Monument Extended" look from early references: it's a paid font. Archivo Expanded gives the same wide,
flat-topped poster voice for £0.

## Materials & texture
Washed black fabric, scratched CD polycarbonate, jewel-case plastic, speaker mesh, photocopy toner, a single red light
leak. Textures live in `brand/textures/` (generated by `design/generators/textures.py`):
`grain-256` (app-wide grain), `scratches-1600` (overlays at 5–10% screen blend), `paper-toner-1024`, `washed-black-512`,
`speaker-mesh-96`.
Rules: texture never sits on top of body text at more than 8% opacity. One light leak per composition.

## Motion
- The signal: carrier line draws, spikes swell with overshoot (`cubic-bezier(.3,1.4,.4,1)`), settle.
- Print registration: red and paper layers misaligned by a few px that snap together.
- The tear: the frame splits along the carrier line to reveal the interface.
- UI timing: micro 140 ms, interface 240 ms, editorial 560 ms. Reduced motion collapses all to ~0.

## Voice
Short. Direct. Music-literate. Sounds like someone who owns too many CDs, not a start-up.

Lines in use: **Music people repeat.** · **Louder things last longer.** · "put it back on." · "side a, side b."

Write: "Import music", "Nothing queued", "This file's format can't be played here."
Never: "Reimagine your sonic journey", "Experience music like never before", "Elevate your listening", emoji in UI,
fake user counts, fake reviews, awards we haven't won.

## Photography & artwork direction
Real objects, low light, hard single source, red practical light. Worn surfaces, fingerprints, dust. No stock
headphones-on-a-model, no neon grids, no floating 3D blobs. Screenshots are shown on black with generous margins,
unrotated, never inside fake device frames with reflections.

## Screenshots
1440×900 or 2560×1600 captures of the real app with real (or clearly synthetic test) libraries. Crop to the window,
no browser chrome. Do not mock UI that doesn't exist.
