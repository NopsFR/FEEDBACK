# FEEDBACK — press kit

## One line
FEEDBACK is a music player for the records, rips and videos you already own.

## Short description (≈50 words)
FEEDBACK is a local-first music and music-video player for Windows, with a phone app that runs from your own
computer. Point it at your music folder and it builds a proper library — albums, artists, lyrics, playlists — and
plays everything with no account, no ads and no internet required.

## Long description (≈150 words)
FEEDBACK is for people whose music lives in folders: CD rips, Bandcamp downloads, live bootlegs, the FLACs you've
carried between computers for fifteen years. It reads the tags and artwork already in your files, builds a fast
searchable library, and plays it back with gapless transitions, ReplayGain levelling, a ten-band EQ and synced lyrics
from `.lrc` files.

It looks like it belongs next to a stack of jewel cases rather than a dashboard — worn black, paper and a little red,
with album artwork setting the colour of the room.

Your phone connects straight to your computer over your own Wi-Fi. Browse everything at home, save the albums you want
on the phone, and they keep playing with no signal. There's no cloud, no account and no tracking. Nothing is uploaded
anywhere.

## Facts
- Platforms: Windows 10/11 (desktop app), iPhone/Android via the built-in phone app (PWA). Native iOS planned.
- Price: free, personal project.
- Formats: MP3, FLAC, ALAC, AAC/M4A, WAV, AIFF, Ogg Vorbis, Opus; MP4/WebM music videos.
- Privacy: no account, no telemetry, no ads. Library data stays on your devices.
- Built with Tauri (Rust) and React.
